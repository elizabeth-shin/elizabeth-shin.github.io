The Problem

We Dropped the Word "Systems"

Instructional Design was born as Instructional Systems Design. Built from WWII military training and mid-20th century educational psychology, the field was designed to analyze human performance, architect learning ecosystems, and build behavioral frameworks.

Then the corporate e-learning boom hit. The "Systems" got dropped. Designers became content factory workers. And now AI is accelerating the same mistake.

If you use AI only to produce content faster, you are not transforming your practice.

You are accelerating the content factory. The asset may be better. The system behind it is still broken.

The Framework

ARISE: A Systems-First Model

ARISE is a framework for thinking about AI as a systems engineering partner rather than a content generation tool. Each phase puts human judgment first and uses AI to carry the heavy lifting of analysis, simulation, and architecture before a single asset is built.

Think of AI as the calculator. You are the mathematician.

You determine the right equation first. Then you hand the calculation to AI. AI does not know your organizational culture, your constraints, or your real human performance gaps. It cannot architect a system in a vacuum. But when you bring the strategic direction, AI can carry extraordinary load.

A
Assess
Analyze root causes, performance gaps, and organizational context before a single asset exists. AI can crunch KPIs and learner performance data to surface the real operational gaps.
R
Route
Map the strategic path: skills adjacency, competency frameworks, learner journey simulation. AI can cross-reference skill matrices and draft competency maps in minutes.
I
Integrate
Connect systems: data, platforms, governance, culture, and human performance levers. AI can model system dependencies and flag integration gaps before you build.
S
Scaffold
Build adaptive infrastructure. Tools, diagnostics, and ecosystems rather than just modules. AI can simulate learner journeys and stress-test your ecosystem design first.
E
Elevate
Measure, iterate, and evolve. Let AI carry the load so you can architect what is next. AI can analyze performance data and surface patterns for adaptive interventions.
